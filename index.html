FLOODLENS BALTIMORE

Welcome to FloodLens Baltimore

FloodLens is to help residents, community organizations, stakeholders, and public agencies across Baltimore City and the communities within the Baltimore Beltway understand where storms flood streets, homes, and neighborhoods, and who is most exposed.

This tool is part of the Baltimore Social-Environmental Collaborative (BSEC) and the Mid-Atlantic Regional Integrated Sciences and Assessments (MARISA) project, and was developed by Civil Engineering researchers at Morgan State University in Dr. James Hunter's lab.

Every effort has been made to ensure the accuracy of the data presented. It is provided for informational purposes only, and the researchers do not assume any liability arising from its use. Additional data may be obtained from the FEMA Map Service Center.
